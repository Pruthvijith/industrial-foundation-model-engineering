# Synthetic lab records

posttrain/data.py constructs four records per group, covering electric power, pressure, energy and missing information. The local profile creates 24 training groups and 12 test groups. A seeded generator supplies magnitudes, asset IDs, option permutation and a rejected answer. Hashes cover canonical JSON. Original fixtures use Apache-2.0. They contain no employer records or individual identities.

The instruction task is SI unit selection and abstention. It has little lexical diversity. It does not require the model to calculate a numeric answer. The second project's independent arithmetic benchmark measures a different capability and includes deliberately conflicting footer text.
